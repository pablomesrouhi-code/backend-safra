## Trust Hierarchy (ICP: KSA Women)

What Saudi women trust before buying expensive online:

1. **Other Saudi women** — reviews with city names (الرياض، جeddah)
2. **Halal / clean ingredients** — full INCI in Arabic
3. **Phone confirmation call** — team calls within minutes after order
4. **Premium packaging signals** — site design = product quality
5. **COD** — pay when received
6. **Clear returns** — 14 days, no hassle
7. **Snapchat/TikTok UGC** — faces like hers, dialect like hers

## Tone of Voice

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Speak to her dignity | "Fix your smell" |
| Use Gulf feminine dialect lightly | MSA-only corporate tone |
| Evidence → relief | Hype exclamation marks |
| "يدعم" · "يساعد" | "يشفي" · "يعالج" |

- Sage green + soft gold — not cheap pink
